## What the Search Console data says

- `3d modeling`: 29,674 impressions, 0 clicks, average position 10.06. This is the largest opportunity, but the phrase is broad. Target it as **architectural 3D modeling services** and **as-built 3D modeling** to attract qualified buyers.
- `3d laser scanning cincinnati oh`: 18 impressions, 0 clicks, average position 5.00. The dedicated local page is already appearing on page one; improve its authority and click-through rate rather than creating overlapping Cincinnati pages.
- `3d site surveying` and variants: 179 combined impressions, positions 73–75. Add the exact concept naturally to the laser-scanning page and future case studies, but do not create a thin duplicate page yet.
- Matterport training/course searches reflect legacy course content, not the current core business. Do not rebuild course pages unless training becomes a product again.
- Bell Event Centre, Panera and Nippert searches prove that project pages can earn traffic. Rebuild selected legacy project pages as useful case studies rather than restoring every old WordPress URL.
